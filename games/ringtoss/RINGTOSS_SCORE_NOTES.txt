Ring Toss - score detection integration

- Score sensors are generated dynamically from main.gd.
- Each cone reads its points value from niveles.json.
- Each ring can score only once.
- A miss is resolved automatically after tiempo_maximo_tiro.
- Reaching puntaje_maximo ends the level early, matching Gold Can Alley.
- PanelResultados handles score saving and next-level unlocking through SaveManager.
- Restart cleans old rings and generates a new one.

Tunable values in the Main inspector under "Deteccion de score":
- sensor_cono_radio
- sensor_cono_altura
- sensor_cono_offset_y
- sensor_centro_ring_radio_local
- tiempo_confirmacion_score
- tiempo_maximo_tiro
- velocidad_vertical_max_score
